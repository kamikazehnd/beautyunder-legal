Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

BeautyUnder is a personal, single-operator publishing tool that uploads Shorts to the BeautyUnder YouTube channel. This policy explains what it does with data.

What we access

  • The Google account of the channel owner authorizes the YouTube Data API to upload videos, read the channel's own videos and manage playlists.
  • The app does not read viewers' Google accounts, emails, contacts or personal data.

What we collect

  • Public video metadata (views, likes, comments) for the channel's own videos, to decide future content.
  • No analytics about individual viewers, no cookies on this site, no advertising trackers.

How data is stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored on the operator's machine and in GitHub Actions secrets, never in this website and never shared with third parties.
  • Product photos come from Open Beauty Facts (CC BY-SA) and are credited in each video description.

Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared. Google APIs are used only to publish and measure the operator's own videos.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any stored token or data, email the contact below.
